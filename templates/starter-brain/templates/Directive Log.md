# {{title}}

Decisions and instructions as the person who owns the work gave them: word for
word, dated and numbered. Plans, interpretations and status belong in other
notes; this one keeps only their words, so any summary can be checked against
the original.

- Quote exactly. If the words were spoken and transcribed, say so. A
  translation goes on the next line, labelled as one.
- Number entries in one sequence and never reuse a number. Date every entry;
  add the time when the order within a day matters.
- Mark a question as a question ("a question, not a decision").
- Never edit or delete an entry. When a later entry changes an earlier one,
  the later entry names the number it replaces ("replaces 3"), and an agent
  follows the newest entry for what it covers.
- When two entries seem to conflict and neither names the other, ask before
  acting on either.
